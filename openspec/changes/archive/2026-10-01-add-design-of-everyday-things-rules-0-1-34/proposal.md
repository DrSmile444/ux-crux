## Why

A comparison of Don Norman, *The Design of Everyday Things* (Revised and Expanded Edition, 2013) with the catalog. Most reviewable ideas were already covered (`AF01`, `A11R`, `A12R`, `A16R`, `A04R`-`A06R`, `E01`, `E03`, `E07`, `S01`, `IA10`, `PC08`), so the book is added as a citation there. Two gaps remain: natural mapping (`Strong`, supported by Fitts and Seeger, 1953) and sensibility checks on high-consequence values (`Expert practice`).

- **Rejected:** a claim about holding the reward until a secondary step is done (the book text has no such example); capture slips; creeping featurism; levels of processing.
- **Scoped out:** design-process material (Double Diamond, HCD loop, five-user rule, Norman's Law, hill-climbing, root-cause analysis), acoustic signifiers, hardware examples.
